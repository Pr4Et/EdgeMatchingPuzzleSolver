The files are named after the step of operations:
step1 - is the starting configuration shown in Fig.2a.
w - Deduction operation operated by clicking "w".
AI2it - AI step with 2 iterations (one instance), operated by clicking the dot button, entering 2, and choosing the folder with partial solutions (step1_w_h3).
y1 - Reset all but ring L1, operated by clicking the "y" button and entering 1.
d - Logical conjunction with the starting configuration, operated by clicking "d" and choosing the first file.
f - Refreshing the configuration tables, operated by clicking "f".
h1- Nucleation operation operated by clicking "h" and entering 1.
